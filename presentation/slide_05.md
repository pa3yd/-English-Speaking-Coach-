# Slide 05 Why consider BDD?

Source: S1 PDF page 5; S3 versions 1 and 2.

## Learner first
Explain this slide from these keywords: window • oxygen evolution • inert • service life • limits. Wait before showing the model answer.

## Core expression
BDD has useful properties, but it is not suitable for every water.

## Natural concise spoken version
BDD has a wide electrochemical window and a high oxygen evolution potential. It is chemically inert and works as a non-sacrificial anode. These are useful material properties, but they are not treatment guarantees. Life depends on the duty. Very low conductivity, heavy solids, or easily biodegradable water can make BDD a poor choice. We need to check suitability and cost before making a proposal.

## Important vocabulary
- oxygen evolution potential — 析氧电位. This is a material specification, not the device voltage.
- non-sacrificial anode — 非牺牲性阳极. BDD is a non-sacrificial anode, but life depends on duty.
- conductivity — 电导率. Low conductivity may affect suitability.
