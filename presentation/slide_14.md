## Important vocabulary
- cubic meters per day — 立方米每天. What is the flow in cubic meters per day?
- Could you repeat that more slowly? — 请慢一点重复. Could you repeat that more slowly?
- Let me confirm my understanding. — 让我确认是否理解正确. Let me confirm my understanding of your target.
- Let me check with our engineer. — 让我向工程师确认. Let me check with our engineer and reply in writing.
- May I show you a slide? — 我可以展示一页幻灯片吗. May I show you a slide on the product range?
