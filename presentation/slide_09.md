## Core expression
Choose the next step from the missing data, not from a fixed buying sequence.

## Natural concise spoken version
The route can start with a lab evaluation using BK1.0 or BK5.0. PK3.0 can support an on-site test. An ME electrolyzer may be considered for a pilot. Project design and quotation then follow the evidence. Customers do not have to buy every stage. We start where their data stops. A plate buyer may never need a kit, and a kit result may support a pilot discussion.

## Fuller reference after an attempt

At each step, we ask what question still needs an answer. Can the water reach the target? What data do our engineers need? A small test does not automatically prove full-system performance or cost.
