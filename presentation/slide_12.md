## Core expression
We review the water, agree on testing, and then discuss a proposal.

## Natural concise spoken version
First, we understand the wastewater, target, and discharge limit. Then we review COD, ammonia nitrogen, chloride, pH, and flow. We agree on a test scope and sample handling case by case. After testing, we propose a solution and confirm delivery and commercial terms. Price depends on anode area and power. V8 states that we do not provide free equipment or free pilot units.

## Fuller reference after an attempt
First, we understand the wastewater, target, and discharge limit. Then we review COD, ammonia nitrogen, chloride, pH, and flow. We agree on a test scope and sample handling case by case. After testing, we propose a solution and confirm delivery and commercial terms. Price depends on anode area and power. V8 states that we do not provide free equipment or free pilot units.

We also ask for the deadline and site conditions. Support scope is agreed per project. We should not promise sample acceptance or a free test. Next, agree who will provide the water report for review.

## Important vocabulary
- COD — 化学需氧量. What are your current COD and target?
- ammonia nitrogen — 氨氮. Could you share the ammonia nitrogen result?
- chloride — 氯离子. What is the chloride concentration?
- discharge limit — 排放限值. Which discharge limit must you meet?
- daily flow rate — 日流量. What is your daily flow rate?

## Customer questions

- When can you quote?
