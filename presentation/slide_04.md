# Slide 04 How does BDD work?

Source: S1 PDF page 4; S3 versions 1 and 2.

## Learner first

## Core expression
BDD oxidizes pollutants, but the water must be tested before and after treatment.

## Natural concise spoken version
Wastewater enters the cell. Oxidation takes place at the anode and can generate hydroxyl radicals. These can attack organic pollutants. Some reactions happen at the surface. In chloride-rich water, active chlorine can also carry oxidation into the water. That can create chlorinated by-products. We test the treated water again. We do not assume complete mineralization or the same result for every wastewater.

Salt can improve conductivity, but that alone does not make water suitable. Results and cost depend on the water, current density, and time. Some streams need pretreatment. Color removal alone does not prove compliance.

## Transition
These reactions help explain why we consider BDD, and where we need caution.

## Important vocabulary
- electrochemical oxidation — 电化学氧化. We use electrochemical oxidation to treat difficult water.
- hydroxyl radicals — 羟基自由基. Oxidation can generate hydroxyl radicals.
- direct oxidation — 直接氧化. Direct oxidation happens at the anode surface.
- indirect oxidation — 间接氧化. Active chlorine can support indirect oxidation.
- chlorinated by-products — 含氯副产物. We need to evaluate possible chlorinated by-products.
