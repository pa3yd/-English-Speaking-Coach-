## Core expression
We scale from modules to systems using test data.

## Natural concise spoken version
At this level, we offer module units, enclosed electrolyzers, and modular cabinets. The MU724, MU924, and MU1124 units are reaction stacks. The ME21424, ME21824, and ME22224 electrolyzers contain two modules in a PP shell. Cabinets add equipment such as a rectifier, pump, controls, and filtration. We select the product level from the project requirements and test data, rather than from flow rate alone.

## Fuller reference after an attempt
At this level, we offer module units, enclosed electrolyzers, and modular cabinets. The MU724, MU924, and MU1124 units are reaction stacks. The ME21424, ME21824, and ME22224 electrolyzers contain two modules in a PP shell. Cabinets add equipment such as a rectifier, pump, controls, and filtration. We select the product level from the project requirements and test data, rather than from flow rate alone.

Cabinets come in single-cell, dual-cell, four-cell, and eight-cell ranges. The ME design circulation flow is ten cubic meters per hour. That describes circulation, not guaranteed treatment capacity. We must keep those two ideas separate.

## Important vocabulary
- beaker module — 烧杯模组. BK1.0 is a one-liter beaker module.
- electrolyzer — 电解槽. An ME electrolyzer may be considered for a pilot.
- rectifier — 整流器. The cabinet includes a rectifier.
- configuration — 配置. Let our engineer confirm the configuration.
- design circulation flow — 设计循环流量. Circulation flow is not guaranteed treatment capacity.
