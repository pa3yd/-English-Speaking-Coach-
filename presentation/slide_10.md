## Natural concise spoken version
This is a reported case from a wet-process phosphoric acid plant in Guangxi. The raw-water COD was thirty-four thousand milligrams per liter. Several treatment steps came before BDD, including precipitation, defluoridation, UV/Fenton, and biological treatment. At the BDD stage, COD went from three thousand two hundred eighty to one hundred forty-six point eight in twelve minutes. This result belongs to that reported sample series.
