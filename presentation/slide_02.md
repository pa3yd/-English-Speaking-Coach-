## Core expression
Please tell us about your water and the target you need to reach.

## Natural concise spoken version
Customers may come to us with high COD, organics that are hard to biodegrade, or a process that cannot reach its treatment target. Some streams also contain a lot of salt. Membrane reject and MVR concentrate are examples worth reviewing. These are starting signals, not proof that BDD will work. First, we ask about the water and the existing process.

## Fuller reference after an attempt
Customers may come to us with high COD, organics that are hard to biodegrade, or a process that cannot reach its treatment target. Some streams also contain a lot of salt. We ask where the wastewater comes from and which limit is still being missed. Salt changes the chemistry and possible by-products. We review the analysis before deciding whether a test makes sense.
