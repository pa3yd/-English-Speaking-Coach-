## Core expression
Could you tell me about your water first?

## Natural concise spoken version
Visitor: What is this machine?
Krystal: It's a BDD electrode system for challenging industrial wastewater.
Visitor: What kind of wastewater?
Krystal: We review high-COD water and organics that are hard to biodegrade. What's your main problem?
Visitor: Our chemical plant has high COD.
Krystal: Could we review your water data and discuss whether a test makes sense?

## Fuller reference after an attempt

Visitor: Can you tell me the price?
Krystal: We first need to understand the application and equipment scope. What are your current COD and target?
Visitor: I'll need to check.
Krystal: That's fine. Could you send the latest water report after the show? Then we can agree on a technical review.
