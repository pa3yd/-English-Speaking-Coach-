## Core expression
BDD is a conductive diamond coating used as an anode.

## Natural concise spoken version
BDD stands for boron-doped diamond. In simple terms, it is a plate with a very thin diamond film. Adding boron makes the diamond conductive. In this application, BDD works as the anode, the positive electrode where oxidation takes place. V8 shows silicon or niobium substrates. The catalogue also lists a film thickness below ten micrometers and different plate shapes and sizes.
