## Important vocabulary
- oxygen evolution potential — 析氧电位. This is a material specification, not the device voltage.
- refractory organics — 难降解有机物. Does the water contain refractory organics?
- membrane reject — 膜浓水. Do you have a separate membrane reject stream?
- MVR concentrate — MVR浓缩液. We can review your MVR concentrate data.
- landfill leachate — 垃圾渗滤液. V8 includes landfill leachate as a potential application.
