# Six modules

All starting levels are unassessed. User describes basic-to-intermediate speaking; this is self-report, not a certified level.

## Module 1 Daily English
Suggested everyday practice, not inferred history: ordering coffee (“Could I get…?”); asking directions (“How do I get to…?”); scheduling (“Would Tuesday work?”); explaining a problem (“The issue is…”); small talk (“How has your week been?”); recounting a day (“First…, then…”).
Start: “What did you do after work yesterday?” Wait; target 2 understandable sentences. Next difficulty: ask one unexpected follow-up. Avoid inventing the user's routines. Reuse patterns in a new context instead of identical drills.

## Module 2 Boromond Introduction
Goal: explain company, role and product scope in 30–60 seconds. Start: “What does Boromond do?” Use F01,F05–F07. Suggested sentence: “We supply BDD electrodes, testing products, and modular systems.” Ask about a buyer's role before recommending a product.

## Module 3 BDD Technology
Goal: explain coating, anode and oxidation in simple language with a limitation. Start: “How would you explain BDD to a nontechnical visitor?” Use F02–F04,F11–F12 and slides 3–6. Next: “Does high salt always help?” Do not require academic terminology when a simpler accurate answer works.

## Module 4 Product Presentation
Use one `presentation/slide_XX.md` at a time. Retrieval → user explanation → one important correction → one customer question → new context. Entry: “Introduce slide one without reading.” Formal route: 1–12,15. The full script is a reference, not a required memorization target.

## Module 5 Customer Simulation
Use customer_simulation.md. Booth 3–5 minutes; online meeting 5–10 minutes; change only one difficulty at a time. Score evidence of asking useful questions and managing uncertainty, not a fictional conversion.

## Module 6 Learning Progress
End with the protocol in progress_protocol.md. Next session continues one specific target; never invent past errors. A learner can switch modules without completing a fixed course ladder.
