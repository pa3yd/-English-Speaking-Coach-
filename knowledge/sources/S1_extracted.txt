02  ·  THE MATERIAL Diamond film   →   Conductive   →   Anode   →   Electrode plate
What is BDD?
Boron-doped diamond — a diamond film used as an electrode.
BOROMOND   |   BDD electrochemical oxidation   |   boromond.com 03
▪  A diamond film. Grown on a conductive plate, thinner than 10 µm.
▪  Boron makes it conductive. Small amounts of boron turn diamond 
into an electrode.
▪  It works as the anode. The positive electrode, where oxidation 
happens.
Simple English: a plate with a very thin diamond coating — and the 
coating is designed to stay in place.
Real BDD plates
Plate, disc, mesh and foam forms.
Silicon or niobium substrate.

03  ·  THE MECHANISM Wastewater   →   Anode   →   Oxidation   →   Pollutants   →   Test results
How does BDD work?
BOROMOND   |   BDD electrochemical oxidation   |   boromond.com 04
Wastewater
goes into the cell
Oxidation at the anode
can generate hydroxyl radicals
·OH attacks organics
long chains break down
Treated water
tested again
Direct oxidation
The pollutant reaches the anode surface and 
reacts there. Part of the work is done by ·OH held 
at the surface.
Indirect oxidation
In chloride-rich water, active chlorine carries 
oxidation into the bulk water. It can also form 
chlorinated by-products.
What salt changes
Salt raises conductivity, which helps. It 
does not make every salty water 
suitable.
Be careful with claims: not every pollutant is fully mineralised, and not every wastewater can be treated. Result and cost depend on the water, the current 
density and the time. Some waters also need pre-treatment first.

Wide electrochemical window

Chemically inert

Non-sacrificial anode

What BDD is not
Not the cheapest option per square metre. Not a fit for every water — very low conductivity, heavy solids or easily biodegradable water are usually
