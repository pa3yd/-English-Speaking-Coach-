Boromond BDD Presentation Speaking Scripts

Two speaking versions for Krystal Tan. Practice the message from keywords, then use the short fallback if needed. The original presentation is unchanged.

Source basis: V8 PDF, 15 pages, and the Boromond Chinese catalogue dated 20260521 found beside the attachment. V8 PPTX and speaker notes were not available. Company statements below are source-supported statements, not independently verified performance. Scripts, customer dialogue and practice prompts are suggested language. [NEEDS INTERNAL CONFIRMATION] marks unresolved facts or terms and is not spoken aloud.

Timing and how to use the pack

Formal route: slides 1–12, then slide 15. Cover all 15 slides in practice, but rehearse slides 13–14 separately as scenarios. After slide 12, use its closing transition and advance to slide 15. To enter the optional practice section instead, say: “Now let’s practice a booth conversation.”

Version 1: 948 spoken words; about 8.6–10.0 minutes at 100–115 words per minute plus 24–30 seconds of total pauses. Target: 8–10 minutes.

Version 2: 1395 spoken words; about 12.5–14.4 minutes at 100–115 words per minute plus 24–30 seconds of total pauses. Target: 12–15 minutes.

Counts include scripts and one transition per formal slide. They exclude headings, coaching, pronunciation notes, Chinese, fallback lines and slides 13–14. Numbers written in words approximate speaking effort; rehearse with a timer because model names and technical numbers may slow you down. Do not rush to force a time target.

For each version: read a sentence, look away, and say the idea. Then use only the memory keywords. Stress the CAPITALIZED syllable in the pronunciation hints; these are simple aids, not phonetic notation. Read model codes as letters plus numbers. Say COD as C O D; say NH3-N as ammonia nitrogen in customer conversation.

Slide 3  What is BDD?

Source: V8 PDF page 3. Formal presentation.

Suggested spoken script: BDD stands for boron-doped diamond. In simple terms, it is a plate with a very thin diamond film. Adding boron makes the diamond conductive. In this application, BDD works as the anode, the positive electrode where oxidation takes place. V8 shows silicon or niobium substrates. The catalogue also lists a film thickness below ten micrometers and different plate shapes and sizes.

Memory keywords: diamond film • boron • conductive • anode • substrate

Transition: Let's look at what happens when the water enters the cell.

If you forget: BDD is a conductive diamond coating used as an anode.

Difficult words and pronunciation: boron: BOR-on; niobium: nye-OH-bee-um; substrate: SUB-strayt; anode: AN-ohd; micrometer: MY-kroh-mee-ter

中文理解: 区分金刚石薄膜与基材。不是整块天然金刚石，也不是永不脱落的保证。

Slide 4  How does BDD work?

Source: V8 PDF page 4. Formal presentation.

Suggested spoken script: Wastewater enters the cell. Oxidation takes place at the anode and can generate hydroxyl radicals. These can attack organic pollutants. Some reactions happen at the surface. In chloride-rich water, active chlorine can also carry oxidation into the water. That can create chlorinated by-products. We test the treated water again. We do not assume complete mineralization or the same result for every wastewater.

Memory keywords: anode • radicals • chloride • by-products • retest

Transition: These reactions help explain why we consider BDD, and where we need caution.

If you forget: BDD oxidizes pollutants, but the water must be tested before and after treatment.

Difficult words and pronunciation: hydroxyl radical: hy-DROK-sil RAD-ih-kul; chloride: KLOR-ide; mineralization: min-er-ul-ih-ZAY-shun

中文理解: 直接与间接氧化并存；含氯水要关注副产物，不能把褪色等同于完全矿化。

Slide 5  Why consider BDD?

Source: V8 PDF page 5. Formal presentation.

Suggested spoken script: BDD has a wide electrochemical window and a high oxygen evolution potential. It is chemically inert and works as a non-sacrificial anode. These are useful material properties, but they are not treatment guarantees. Life depends on the duty. Very low conductivity, heavy solids, or easily biodegradable water can make BDD a poor choice. We need to check suitability and cost before making a proposal.

Memory keywords: window • oxygen evolution • inert • service life • limits

Transition: With those limits in mind, here are some applications we can review.

If you forget: BDD has useful properties, but it is not suitable for every water.

Difficult words and pronunciation: inert: ih-NURT; sacrificial: sak-ruh-FISH-ul; evolution: ev-uh-LOO-shun

中文理解: 材料优点不等于处理承诺。电位数字不是设备运行电压，测量条件待确认。

Accuracy note: [NEEDS INTERNAL CONFIRMATION] Reference electrode, electrolyte, pH and measurement method for the potential and window figures. This marker is not spoken.
