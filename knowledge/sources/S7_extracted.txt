Boromond BDD Seven Day Speaking Practice Plan

A gradual plan for a Chinese international sales professional. Spend 20–30 minutes each day. The aim is to explain the message accurately from keywords, ask useful questions, and handle uncertainty calmly.

Use the V8 PDF, the two script versions, the role plays and the vocabulary workbook. No audio recording is included. Read aloud and listen back to your own phone recording. If a trusted colleague can model a sentence, listen once and repeat naturally. Avoid guessing pronunciation for product names; confirm the preferred company pronunciation internally.

Day 1  Understand the story

Read or listen: Read V1 slides 1–3 aloud slowly. Use the PDF to connect the words with the pictures.

20 to 30 minute routine: 4 min read; 8 min repeat in short phrases; 6 min speak from keywords; 4 min record and review. Total 22 min.

Speak without the full script: Introduce Boromond and explain BDD in 60–90 seconds using only five keywords.

Completion criterion: Record one take; include the company, customer water, conductive diamond film and anode, with no more than two script checks.

Day 2  Explain with limits

Read or listen: Read V1 slides 4–6 and the matching vocabulary topics Materials and Mechanism.

20 to 30 minute routine: 5 min read; 8 min repeat difficult phrases; 7 min explain without script; 4 min review. Total 24 min.

Speak without the full script: Explain how BDD works and name one reason a water may be unsuitable.

Completion criterion: Speak for 90 seconds; include oxidation, a chloride by-product caution, and a suitability limit; make no universal removal claim.

Day 3  Know the product roles

Read or listen: Read V1 slides 7–9. Check the product pictures in the source PDF.

20 to 30 minute routine: 5 min read; 7 min practice product names and volumes; 8 min answer three product questions; 4 min review. Total 24 min.

Speak without the full script: Choose a discussion level for a lab user, a plant tester, and a cell builder. State what you still need to know.

Completion criterion: Correctly distinguish BK1.0, BK5.0, PK3.0, MU and ME; say BK5.0 is one liter and PK3.0 two to five liters; do not call circulation flow treatment capacity.

Day 4  Tell cases honestly

Read or listen: Read V1 slides 10–12 and 15, then read the fuller case scripts in V2.

20 to 30 minute routine: 5 min read; 7 min numbers aloud; 8 min retell both cases; 5 min compare with source. Total 25 min.

Speak without the full script: Explain each reported case in 60 seconds and state a limitation.

Completion criterion: Keep the BDD stage at 3280 to 146.8 in 12 minutes; state the three-hour energy figure as case-specific; include a practical next step.

Day 5  Practice the booth

Read or listen: Read the trade show dialogue and the poor-fit branch. Practice slide 13 separately.

20 to 30 minute routine: 4 min read; 8 min alternate roles; 7 min respond to price and poor fit; 5 min record. Total 24 min.

Speak without the full script: Run a three-minute booth conversation without reading full lines. Use a partner or read the customer prompts, then look away to answer.

Completion criterion: Ask at least four useful water questions, avoid a price guess, identify a possible poor fit, and agree on one data-review action.

Day 6  Handle an online meeting

Read or listen: Read the online dialogue and questions 1–6. Skim questions 7–13. Practice slide 14 separately.

20 to 30 minute routine: 5 min read; 8 min answer eight shuffled questions; 7 min opening and summary; 5 min review. Total 25 min.

Speak without the full script: Open a meeting, answer eight customer questions, and summarize the missing information.

Completion criterion: Answer at least eight prompts; use a safe fallback for unknown settings or terms; identify who sends what next and when to confirm the review.

Day 7  Deliver and assess

Read or listen: Read only memory keywords for V1 slides 1–12 and 15. Review V2 expansion on two difficult slides.

20 to 30 minute routine: 3 min warm-up; 8–10 min V1 recording; 5 min review; 5 min repeat two weak slides; 3 min question practice. Total 24–26 min.

Speak without the full script: Deliver the formal presentation without reading the full script. Leave slides 13–14 for separate role-play.

Completion criterion: Record the actual time, no more than five script checks, accurate product roles and numbers, two limitations, and a clear closing next step. If slower than ten minutes, note it honestly and repeat tomorrow rather than rushing.

What to do after day seven

Repeat the two weakest days. Move to V2 after you can deliver V1 clearly without frequent reading. Build toward 12–15 minutes at your actual pace. Ask a colleague to listen for technical accuracy and clear questions, rather than accent alone. Memorize the five-step customer route before adding more vocabulary.
