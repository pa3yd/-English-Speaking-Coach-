# Slide 04 How does BDD work?

## Core expression
BDD oxidizes pollutants, but the water must be tested before and after treatment.

## Natural concise spoken version
Wastewater enters the cell. Oxidation takes place at the anode and can generate hydroxyl radicals. These can attack organic pollutants. Some reactions happen at the surface. In chloride-rich water, active chlorine can also carry oxidation into the water. That can create chlorinated by-products. We test the treated water again. We do not assume complete mineralization or the same result for every wastewater.

# Slide 05 Why consider BDD?

## Core expression

## Natural concise spoken version

We need to check suitability and cost before making a proposal.
